# Packaged scope

This repository preserves the autonomous trading research and both completed validation phases. It is separate from the earlier Momentum simulation application.

Included: original source patches, paper guard and coordinator, data and signal adapters, portable configuration/bootstrap tools, meaningful offline tests, exact upstream and dependency pins, research, source ledger, reusable-code catalog, PRD, operational instructions, and sanitized results.

Excluded: brokerage credentials and account identifiers; personal financial declarations, application emails and PDFs; live/private configuration; session and authentication artifacts; screenshots of account surfaces; raw broker executions, market history and runtime journals; conversation transcripts; unrelated WorkOS and finance files; virtual environments, compiled binaries, downloaded upstream clones and caches. Local originals are preserved.

Synthetic fixtures exist only for offline tests and carry fictional identifiers. The broker evidence summary describes the actual paper exercise, with identifying fields omitted. No claim relies on presenting synthetic outcomes as real broker outcomes.

The local publication review and its detailed file/hash manifest remain outside this repository. The compact result documentation here describes software evidence, not private workflow or account review records.
